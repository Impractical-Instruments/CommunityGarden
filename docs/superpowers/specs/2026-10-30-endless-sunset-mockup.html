What should a flailing person look like?

Each sketch loops through the whole arc: empty room (slow, dim smolder), someone standing (wakes up), then flailing (squiggles, with the rings and rays rippling around them). The person is a stand-in figure; the real one comes from the depth camera. The three only differ in how the body is drawn.

A. Wiggly outline

A white figure with an ink outline, like the people in the concept art. The outline turns into a jittery sine squiggle as they move.

B. Squiggle-filled body

The body is filled with stacked sine-wave lines, like the website's squiggle, and they wave harder the more you move.

C. Squiggle echoes

A white figure with squiggly contour lines radiating outward from them, like the sun's rings but coming off the person.
