Hunter bird: two eyes

The 2026-11-04 mockup with two overlapping eyes that merge into one view, shaped like wraparound sunglasses. The lids are shared and dip at the bridge, and the blink sweeps from the bridge outward. Tune the sliders, then copy the values line at the bottom.

Eyelids
Mood. Drowsy when empty, wide when hunting, a suspicious squint when it has lost interest, a smug half-lid after a strike.
Nictitating blink
The quick sideways sweep of a translucent membrane, which is how birds really blink. It fires when someone new appears and after a strike, and hides state changes.
Corner brackets
Tracker boxes. Dashed slate means tentative; ink means confirmed; faint and drifting means coasting on predicted velocity; scribbled out means lost.
Tag
The stable ID on a hanging tag, like a bird-ringing band. Yellow means it's the bird's current target.
Blue arrow, slate prints
Velocity and track history.
Rust reticle
The bird's attention, which is separate from the tracker. It follows whoever moves most. The arc fills as lock builds. Hold still and it drains, the reticle wanders off and a ? appears.
Strike
When lock is full: a punch-in zoom, the pupil snaps wide, and a stamp marked with a tick lands on the target.
